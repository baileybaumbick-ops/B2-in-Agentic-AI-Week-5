You turn one source document into a small set of wiki concept notes for an Obsidian vault.

Pick the 4 to 6 most important concepts, frameworks, or company cases in the source.

Titles are the note's filename, so they must be short noun phrases naming the idea:
- 1 to 4 words, Title Case.
- Name the concept, not the section. Do NOT copy the source's section headings.
- For a company case, use "<Company> Case", e.g. "Intel Case", "Tesla Case".
- Never use a sentence, a question, a verb phrase, a lecture number, "Class", "Notes", "Summary", file names, or IDs.
- If an existing note title already names the same concept, reuse that exact title.
- Never reuse an existing case title for a different company's story. A case about NutraSweet and Holland Sweetener is not the "Intel Case"; name each case after its own company or case name (e.g. "Bitter Competition Case").

Good titles: "Added Value", "Irreplaceability", "Five Forces", "Barriers to Entry", "Counter-Positioning", "Entry Deterrence", "Network Effects", "Intel Case".
Bad titles: "Irreplaceability Drives Value Capture" (a sentence), "Defining Added Value" (a verb phrase), "Case - Intel" (copied heading), "Class 3 Notes" (lecture name).

For each concept:
- topic: choose the best folder from the allowed list. Company cases go in "Company Cases".
- summary: 1 to 2 sentences in plain language, faithful to the source.
- key_points: 3 to 5 short paraphrased facts from the source. Keep numbers exactly as written. For each point, give the section heading it came from, copied exactly from the allowed section list.
- related: up to 3 titles of other concepts, from this batch or the existing titles, that a reader should visit next.

Do not add facts that are not in the source.
