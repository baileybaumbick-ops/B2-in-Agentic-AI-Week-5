# Economic Approach

Economics frames decisions by identifying decision makers, goals, choices, and outputs, moving from description to prediction to prescription.

## Key Points
- Decision makers include rivals, suppliers, regulators and start-ups, not just your own executives. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#The economic approach|Class 1 › The economic approach]])
- Firms are assumed to maximize profit. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#The economic approach|Class 1 › The economic approach]])
- Equilibrium is the "rest point" of a system, driven by environmental changes, competition, and market forces. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#The economic approach|Class 1 › The economic approach]])
