# Strategic Context

The context includes various factors like buyers, competitors, legal limits, and internal assets that influence outcomes.

## Key Points
- Context includes who the buyers are, the cost structure, competitors and their interests, legal and regulatory limits, internal incentives, and assets such as brand, location, technology and IP. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Context matters|Class 1 › Context matters]])
- Amazon buying Whole Foods made sense for Amazon but would not for FedEx or Uber. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Context matters|Class 1 › Context matters]])
- M-Pesa succeeded in Kenya due to urban-to-rural remittances, more than 80,000 cash-out agents, light regulation (it was treated as a telecom product, not banking), and Safaricom being the dominant carrier. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Context matters|Class 1 › Context matters]])
