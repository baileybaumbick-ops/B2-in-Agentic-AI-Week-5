# Strategy

Strategy is a plan to achieve a firm's goal by establishing a persistent difference over rivals.

## Key Points
- Strategy is a plan of action to achieve the firm's goal (profit) that establishes a persistent difference over rivals. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Defining strategy|Class 1 › Defining strategy]])
- Strategy requires dissecting context and trade-offs to understand how others will respond and which features of the environment matter. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Defining strategy|Class 1 › Defining strategy]])
- Bad strategy is different from bad luck. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Defining strategy|Class 1 › Defining strategy]])
