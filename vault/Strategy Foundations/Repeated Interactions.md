# Repeated Interactions

Repeated interactions allow firms to build reputations and cooperate by threatening retaliation over the long term.

## Key Points
- Repetition lets players build a reputation and cooperate by threatening retaliation. ([[Class 8 - Strategic and Repeated Interactions#Repeated interactions and reputation|Class 8 › Repeated interactions and reputation]])
- Firms often interact for years to build reputation. ([[Class 8 - Strategic and Repeated Interactions#Repeated interactions and reputation|Class 8 › Repeated interactions and reputation]])
- Long-term players can sustain cooperation by splitting markets. ([[Class 8 - Strategic and Repeated Interactions#Repeated interactions and reputation|Class 8 › Repeated interactions and reputation]])
