# Trade-Offs and Opportunity Cost

Firms must manage trade-offs where moving one goal affects another, and opportunity cost is the value of the best alternative foregone.

## Key Points
- Firms control operating decisions that move both revenue and cost, usually in opposite directions. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Trade-offs and opportunity cost|Class 1 › Trade-offs and opportunity cost]])
- Examples include Netflix's password-sharing crackdown, Apple's App Tracking Transparency, and Disney launching streaming and cannibalizing cable. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Trade-offs and opportunity cost|Class 1 › Trade-offs and opportunity cost]])
- Disney gave up more than $300 million a year in licensing fees when it pulled content from Netflix for Disney+. ([[Class 1 - Introduction to Strategy#Trade-offs and opportunity cost|Class 1 › Trade-offs and opportunity cost]])
