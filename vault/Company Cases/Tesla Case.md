# Tesla Case

Tesla demonstrated how learning-curve and scale effects can overcome initial financial disadvantages, leveraging technological advantages to overcome high entry barriers.

## Key Points
- The Model S was Tesla's first real production car and the first entry by a new US carmaker since WWII. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Case - Tesla|Class 6 › Case - Tesla]])
- Learning-curve and scale effects could make the numbers much better than they looked for a start-up. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Case - Tesla|Class 6 › Case - Tesla]])
- Tesla overcame high barriers by building its brand and selling directly instead of through dealers. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Case - Tesla|Class 6 › Case - Tesla]])
- Tesla's durable advantage may lie in software, batteries, and charging rather than car making. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Case - Tesla|Class 6 › Case - Tesla]])
