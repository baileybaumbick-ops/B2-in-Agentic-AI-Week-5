# Entry Barriers

Barriers to entry make a market difficult to enter or less profitable for new competitors. These barriers can include economies of scale, scarce resources, and intellectual property.

## Key Points
- Barriers include economies of scale relative to market size, scarce resources, and learning curves. ([[Class 4 - Industry Analysis#Entry and barriers to entry|Class 4 › Entry and barriers to entry]])
- Direct network effects and indirect platform network effects exist in the context of entry. ([[Class 4 - Industry Analysis#Entry and barriers to entry|Class 4 › Entry and barriers to entry]])
- Intellectual property, such as patents, is crucial in industries like pharmaceuticals. ([[Class 4 - Industry Analysis#Entry and barriers to entry|Class 4 › Entry and barriers to entry]])
