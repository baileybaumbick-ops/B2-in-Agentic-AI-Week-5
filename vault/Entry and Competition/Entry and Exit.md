# Entry and Exit

Entry and exit are common occurrences where entrants and exiting firms are generally smaller than established firms. Entry and exit rates vary significantly across different industries.

## Key Points
- Entrants and exiting firms tend to be smaller than established firms. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Facts about entry and exit|Class 6 › Facts about entry and exit]])
- Those that survive grow quickly. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Facts about entry and exit|Class 6 › Facts about entry and exit]])
- Entry is broader than new firms, including new markets and new products. ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Facts about entry and exit|Class 6 › Facts about entry and exit]])
- Entry includes new markets (M-Pesa entering India, Amazon entering grocery) and new products (Ozempic, Vanilla Coke, the microprocessor). ([[Class 6 - Entry and Positioning#Facts about entry and exit|Class 6 › Facts about entry and exit]])
