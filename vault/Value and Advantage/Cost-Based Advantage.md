# Cost-Based Advantage

Cost-based advantage is achieved by optimizing costs, such as using data or strategic investments to achieve operational excellence.

## Key Points
- Chevron used sensors and machine learning for predictive maintenance on about 5,000 heat exchangers, saving millions. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Three sources of competitive advantage|Class 5 › Three sources of competitive advantage]])
- AMD invested in PC and server CPUs and graphics despite conventional wisdom, paying $325 million to exit a contract. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Three sources of competitive advantage|Class 5 › Three sources of competitive advantage]])
