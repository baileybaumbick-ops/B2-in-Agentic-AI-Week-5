# Niche-Based Advantage

Niche-based advantage involves finding a subset of customers where price rivalry is reduced, often through differentiation.

## Key Points
- Horizontal differentiation means customers disagree about the ideal product, such as in music or fashion. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Three sources of competitive advantage|Class 5 › Three sources of competitive advantage]])
- Vertical differentiation means customers agree on a product but differ on the price they will pay for quality. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Three sources of competitive advantage|Class 5 › Three sources of competitive advantage]])
- Kam Pek Casino targets low rollers by using live dealers on video, keeping labor cost 30% below other casinos. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Three sources of competitive advantage|Class 5 › Three sources of competitive advantage]])
