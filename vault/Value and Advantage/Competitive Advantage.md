# Competitive Advantage

Competitive advantage is the ability to be consistently more profitable than rivals in a market. It means being irreplaceable in a way that differs from competitors.

## Key Points
- Competitive advantage is the ability to be consistently more profitable than competitors in the same market or industry. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Definition|Class 5 › Definition]])
- A firm with a competitive advantage is irreplaceable in a way that differs from its rivals. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Definition|Class 5 › Definition]])
- A value proposition is a short, precise statement of how the firm intends to create value, its plan for opening the gap between benefit B and cost C. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#Value proposition|Class 5 › Value proposition]])
- Assets enable activities, and activities create competitive advantage when they fit the context. ([[Class 5 - Competitive Advantage#The AAA framework|Class 5 › The AAA framework]])
